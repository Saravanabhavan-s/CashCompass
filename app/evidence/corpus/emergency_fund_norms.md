# Emergency fund planning norms

Emergency fund months of expenses sizing: common planning guidance is 3 to 6 months of essential expenses, 6 to 12 months for single-income households or irregular income. This is practitioner guidance, not regulation. Emergency fund months of expenses before investing: build the buffer before taking market risk. Job loss: hold the buffer in instruments with same-day or next-day access such as savings accounts, sweep-in fixed deposit and liquid funds. Guaranteed return fixed deposit emergency corpus ladders can be used for the portion not needed immediately.
