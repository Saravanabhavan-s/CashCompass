# Income tax: 80C, TDS and capital gains

Tax saving 80C: Section 80C allows deductions up to Rs 1.5 lakh a year under the old regime for PPF, ELSS and tax saver fixed deposits. Fixed deposit real return after inflation tax TDS: interest is taxed at slab rate and banks deduct TDS above the annual threshold, so real return after inflation can be thin or negative. Equity long term capital gains tax growth: long term gains on listed equity are taxed at a concessional rate above an annual exemption; rates have changed in recent Budgets, so verify the current rate.
