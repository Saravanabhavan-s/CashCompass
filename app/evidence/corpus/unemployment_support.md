# Unemployment support schemes

Unemployment benefit scheme still valid eligibility revisions: the Employees State Insurance Corporation runs an unemployment allowance for insured persons (Atal Beemit Vyakti Kalyan Yojana), paying a share of average wages for a limited period after involuntary job loss, with contribution-history eligibility. Scheme terms, relaxations and sunset dates have been revised; verify with ESIC before relying on it. Provident fund partial withdrawal after job loss is a separate income smoothing option.
