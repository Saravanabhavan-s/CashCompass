# DICGC deposit insurance

DICGC deposit insurance limit: the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation insures bank deposits (savings, fixed deposit, recurring deposit, current) up to Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank, principal and interest combined. Cover is per bank, not per branch. Fixed deposit and recurring deposit balances count toward the same limit. Source: DICGC Act 1961 as amended 2021; verify current limit at dicgc.org.in.
