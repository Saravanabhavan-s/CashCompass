# Public Provident Fund and small savings

PPF guaranteed return small savings: Public Provident Fund has a 15 year tenure, deposits of Rs 500 to Rs 1.5 lakh a year, sovereign guaranteed return, and qualifies for Section 80C tax saving under the old regime. The rate is notified by the Government of India every quarter. Recent changes revisions small savings scheme rate: quarterly notifications can hold, raise or cut rates; always check the latest Ministry of Finance notification. Partial withdrawal allowed from the 7th financial year.
