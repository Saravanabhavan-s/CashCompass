# DPDP Act 2023 excerpt

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, summary excerpt: processing of personal data requires free, specific, informed consent or a legitimate use; data principals may request correction and erasure of personal data (Section 12); data fiduciaries must limit processing to the stated purpose and delete data when it is no longer needed. Evidence retrieved by this layer is public market and regulatory data and contains no personal data. Summary for orientation only; read the Act for the authoritative text.
